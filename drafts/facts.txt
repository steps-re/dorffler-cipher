Half-pages: 270. From 25%-height strips: 249; from 15%-height strips: 8; kept from the old whole-page reading because the strip read failed a key-free sanity check: 13.
Strip re-read: every half-page cut into 5 overlapping full-resolution strips (25% of page height, 6% overlap), read by Gemini 4 Argon, stitched; the key was never shown to the reader. 1,360 calls, 5 failures. Pilot on 3 pages: 5 strips did as well as 11. A second pass at 11 strips on all of 19/102 plus a 3-reader majority vote changed 134 numbers and moved the fit half from 60.1% to 60.3%: no real gain, the reader is at its plateau.
Why: the whole-page reads misread digits systematically, 8 as 0 (28 read as 20, 18 as 10, 87 as 07). An independent blind read of two pages by a different model family (Claude) agreed with the strip read on 93.7% and 85.7% of numbers, against 91.0% and 80.2% for the old reading.
Whole-page alignment of clerk lines to cipher (analysis/align.py): no gain over the next-two-lines pairing (60.9% vs 62.1% on the old reading's test half), but its shuffled control is 0.3%. Pairing was not the bottleneck.
Key fills (analysis/cloze.py): for each number, its fit-half occurrences are dealt into two disjoint sets; Gemini 4 Argon proposes a value from each set separately; a value is kept only if both agree. Validation by hiding 62 known entries: both sets agreed on 58, 53 of 58 matched C04 (91%).
